## What this policy covers

This policy describes what data this website collects, why, and what happens to it. It covers this website only.

## Data we collect

**Contact form.** If you submit the contact form, we receive what you type: your name, phone number, email address (if provided), company name (if provided), the service you selected, and your message. Submissions are delivered to our own customer-relationship system so we can respond to you. We also record the page you submitted from and standard campaign parameters if you arrived through an ad.

**Instant audit.** When you run the instant audit, the address you enter is sent to Google PageSpeed Insights, which loads that page and returns its own measurements. We keep the address and those measurements so we can follow up. If you then ask for the full breakdown, we also receive the name, WhatsApp number and email you enter, and we use them to contact you about it.

**WhatsApp.** If you choose to contact us on WhatsApp, that conversation happens on WhatsApp and is governed by WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy. We only receive what you send us there.

**Application forms.** The "become our client" and "apply to jobs & internships" buttons open forms hosted by Airtable on airtable.com. Nothing is sent to Airtable unless you open one of those forms and submit it. What you enter there is received by us and is also processed by Airtable as our forms provider, under Airtable's own terms and privacy policy.

**Embedded Instagram content.** The homepage shows preview images of selected posts from our Instagram account. These previews are served from this website and involve no Instagram connection. If you click one, the official Instagram player loads for that post and Instagram may set its own cookies from that point — governed by Instagram's own terms and privacy policy. Nothing loads from Instagram before you click.

## What we do not do

We do not sell your data. We do not share form submissions with third parties beyond the systems we use to receive and respond to them.

## How long we keep data

Form submissions are kept for as long as needed to handle your inquiry and any business relationship that follows.

To ask what data we hold about you, or to request correction or deletion, contact us at the email address below.
